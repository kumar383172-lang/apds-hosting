Advanced Public Distribution Platform

The Advanced Public Distribution System (APDS) is a modern web-based solution developed to improve the efficiency, transparency, and accessibility of the public ration distribution process. This system digitizes beneficiary registration, ration card management, and distribution tracking.


Our mission is to eliminate manual paperwork, reduce corruption, minimize human errors, and provide a secure and reliable platform for both citizens and administrators. With responsive design and interactive features, the project delivers a smooth and user-friendly digital experience.
