Portal Introduction

The Advanced Public Distribution System (APDS) is a digital platform designed to improve transparency and efficiency in ration distribution.


  • ✔ Helps citizens report issues related to ration distribution.
  • ✔ Provides a transparent and user-friendly grievance system.
  • ✔ Reduces the need to visit government offices physically.
  • ✔ Improves monitoring of food distribution and stock management.
  • ✔ Enables quick complaint tracking and status updates.
  • ✔ Ensures fair and efficient service delivery.
